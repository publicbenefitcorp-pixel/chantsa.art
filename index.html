About the Artist

A practice shaped by observation, erosion, and atmosphere

Based between the coast and the city, Elena Maris develops paintings that sit between abstraction and landscape. Her work begins with field sketches, fragments of writing, and collected color notes, then evolves in the studio through repeated layering and removal. The resulting surfaces carry traces of place while remaining open, quiet, and unresolved in compelling ways.

Her paintings are held in private collections and have been exhibited in independent project spaces, regional galleries, and curated group exhibitions focused on contemporary material practice.

Mediums

Oil, acrylic, charcoal, chalk, cold wax, and mixed media on linen, canvas, and paper.

Artist Statement
“I paint to stay with uncertainty long enough for an image to become felt before it becomes known.”

My paintings begin with an interest in residue: what remains after a place has been left, after a season has shifted, after an emotion has quieted into atmosphere. I build surfaces slowly, letting color settle and recede. Marks are added, buried, softened, and sometimes removed altogether.

Rather than describe a specific landscape or room, the work seeks a threshold between recognition and ambiguity. I want the viewer to enter through mood, temperature, rhythm, and memory.
